# 【001】Starryear-Abstract-Quarter

Create one seamless 1:2 vertical artwork from one photograph. Keep the actual source crop in Panel 1, then produce three sibling interpretations in one restrained editorial mass-field language: large simplified forms, mostly controlled edges, a uniform ivory field, sparse structural lines, source-derived color, tactile matte surfaces, and generous visual silence. Preserve the source's strongest recognizable backbone when it carries the work—such as a trunk-to-root spread, an architectural span, a body gesture, or a path direction—while transforming it through scale, omission, planar fusion, rhythm, and impossible physical behavior. Panels 2–4 contain no photographic pixels or realistic redraws, but they may remain clearly traceable to the photographed subject.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the source and lock three to six traceable facts: dominant subject, principal axis, depth order, interval or rhythm, brightest opening, color roles, and meaningful negative space.
2. Choose one least-destructive 2:1 crop. Use its real pixels in Panel 1 and the same crop only as evidence for Panels 2–4; never regenerate Panel 1.
3. Choose the material response from visible evidence. When the source is dominated by backlit foliage, translucent membranes, fine branching, or luminous gaps, activate **Plant-Light Permeability**: keep the shared editorial mass-field grammar, use mostly flat translucent planes with crisp-to-gently-soft boundaries, slightly smaller occupation (about 58–76% width and 40–60% height), and a larger role for sparse structural lines. Keep clustered interval marks minimal so they never resemble ink dots or brush texture. Do not activate it merely because plants are present; the light-through-material relationship must organize the photograph.
4. Build a recognizability rhythm rather than a one-way erasure ladder. Panel 2 condenses the subject and may keep its dominant silhouette family plus spatial scaffolding; Panel 3 loosens identity through relational displacement; Panel 4 may recover an expressive source-derived gesture as an afterimage, provided it is materially transformed rather than literally redrawn.
5. Generate Panel 2 as **Condensed Motif**: on a uniform warm-ivory field, compress dominant masses, axes, gaps, and selected spatial context into one centered construction occupying about 64–84% of the panel width and 47–68% of its height. Preserve enough of the source backbone for immediate kinship, then interrupt, fuse, simplify, or flatten it so it reads as authored abstraction rather than a miniature realistic scene.
6. Generate Panel 3 as **Relational Drift**: reuse Panel 2's palette, mark family, scale, and restraint, then loosen identity from position. Recombine one recognizable structural echo and two source relationships through split-and-rejoin, nested gaps, rhythmic displacement, subtraction, or controlled overlap. Add only shallow depth; it must remain Panel 2's close sibling, not a new 3D style.
7. Generate Panel 4 as **Physical Afterimage**: keep the same mass-field grammar and apply one source-derived impossible behavior—such as upward release, gravity reversal, inside/outside exchange, liquefaction, suspension, or self-passage. A simplified source gesture may remain recognizable when it carries the emotional force; dissolve its edges, intervals, or continuity enough that the result feels like an afterimage rather than a depiction.
8. Generate Panels 2–4 separately. Assemble them deterministically beneath Panel 1 as four equal 2:1 panels, defaulting to 1600 × 3200 px, without seams, frames, or overlays.
9. Inspect at full size and thumbnail. Do not reject a panel merely because the source subject remains recognizable. Reject it only when recognizability comes from literal tracing, realistic detail, copied photographic rendering, or untransformed scenery rather than selective reconstruction. Also reject generic surreal symbols and unrelated decoration.
10. Return exactly one completed quartet plus one brief note naming the retained facts, light invariant, emotional invariant, and abstraction progression.

## Guardrails

- Panel 1 allows only cropping, color-profile conversion, and technical resizing; no generation, retouching, recoloring, relighting, denoising, sharpening, object removal, or texture.
- Panels 2–4 must share one strict visual grammar: uniform warm-ivory ground, restrained source palette, one dominant simplified mass family, clean matte surface with only faint tactile variation, sparse geometric structural lines, very limited small marks, generous negative space, and comparable centered visual weight. Panel 3 or 4 fails if it introduces glossy 3D rendering, architectural cutaways, cinematic scenery, dense fragmentation, or a different mark vocabulary.
- Balance four equal priorities: surreal through impossible but coherent relationships; abstract through nonliteral form; minimal through disciplined element count and visual silence; artistic through deliberate composition, tension, rhythm, and emotion rather than generic icons or decoration.
- Panels 2–4 must lean toward contemporary editorial cut-paper, dry print, or restrained screen-print abstraction rather than traditional ink painting. Keep most major silhouette edges clean and controlled, but allow limited dry, broken, or softly abraded edges and short released marks when they express source movement. No wet ink wash, calligraphic smearing, pooling, blooms, splashes, or diffuse bleeding.
- Give the abstract forms refined material texture and high image fidelity without changing the established style or adding visual clutter.
- In Plant-Light Permeability mode, translucency is structural rather than atmospheric: light passes through flat source-colored planes while the ivory ground stays flat and unlit. Overlaps should read as clean layered printing, not wet pigment. Preserve diagonal force, occlusion order, luminous gaps, and clustered intervals; reject botanical illustration, intact leaf silhouettes, watercolor bloom, gauzy fog, or decorative scatter.
- At thumbnail size, Panels 2–4 must read as three related interpretations by the same hand: Panel 2 as condensed evidence, Panel 3 as displaced relation, and Panel 4 as a released physical afterimage. Kinship may come from both the recognizable source backbone and the shared palette, scale, edge behavior, spacing, and line weight.
- Use selective contour retention: Panel 2 may preserve the dominant source silhouette family and simplified spatial context; Panel 3 should interrupt or displace it; Panel 4 may recover one bold gestural contour or directional spread, but not the complete photographic outline and detail set.
- Prohibit photographic fragments, photomontage, double exposure, filter effects, and realistic redraws. Recognizable source-derived silhouettes are allowed when simplified, recomposed, materially translated, and integrated into the abstraction.
- Every important mark must trace to a source fact. Abstraction may enlarge, fold, fuse, hollow, repeat, suspend, invert, penetrate, or change the physical behavior of source relationships. It may invent transformations, but not unrelated subjects, stock surreal symbols, arbitrary circles, grids, portals, planets, eyes, or unsupported colors.
- For people, animals, architecture, and culturally specific objects, preserve only the source-derived relations allowed by the abstraction ladder; never fabricate hidden faces, bodies, structural detail, or symbolic replacements.
- The artwork contains no text, numbers, labels, dates, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, borders, gaps, shadows, rounded corners, or mockup effects.
