# 【001】Starryear-Abstract-Quarter Full Prompt

Treat one user-uploaded photograph as both immutable photographic evidence and the sole content, spatial, color, and material source for the three lower panels. Create a strict 1:2 vertical quartet: the real source crop at the top, followed by three stages in one restrained mass-field language with a recognizability rhythm of condensed identity, relational drift, and gestural release. Recognizability is not the problem; photographic copying, mechanical tracing, and untranslated realistic detail are. Panel 2 may preserve the dominant subject backbone and simplified space; Panel 3 separates identity from position; Panel 4 may release one strong source-derived gesture, radial direction, or growth action so the subject remains traceable as an afterimage. Unity comes from the source backbone, palette, scale, edges, line weight, density, and negative space. Avoid traditional wet ink-wash character, while permitting limited dry broken print-like edges and released short marks; exclude pooling, bleeding, splashing, and broad ink diffusion.

## 1. Input and source lock

- Accept one primary photograph. Choose the least destructive 2:1 crop while protecting the principal subject, key relationships, visual entrance, important negative space, and brightest opening. Never stretch it.
- Panel 1 must use the real cropped pixels. Permit only color-profile conversion and technical resizing; prohibit generation, redrawing, color grading, retouching, object removal or addition, relighting, denoising, sharpening, and texture overlays.
- Panels 2–4 may extract evidence only from the locked crop. Do not change viewpoint or borrow subjects, compositions, or palettes from other images.

## 2. Internal observation

Complete this internally without outputting analysis:

1. Lock three to six locatable facts: dominant subject, principal axis or horizon, foreground/middle/background order, key interval or repetition, brightest opening, color roles, and meaningful void.
2. Lock one light invariant: direction, brightest region, and large-scale tonal relationship.
3. Lock one emotional invariant based only on visible evidence, such as coolness, dampness, suspension, warmth, solitude, or ease.
4. Build a relationship map containing at least two identity anchors, one spatial anchor, and one light/color anchor. Identity anchors are not intact outlines; they are the source's most distinctive proportions, adjacencies, gaps, bends, repetitions, or color contacts.
5. Map every proposed shape, line, field, or material flow to a source fact. You may invent how source relationships transform and obey impossible physics, but may not invent a new subject category.
6. Establish a recognizability rhythm: Panel 2 may retain the dominant silhouette family, one spatial scaffold, and two relational anchors; Panel 3 reduces direct recognition through displacement and interruption; Panel 4 may recover one bold source gesture or directional contour, but must remove photographic detail and transform continuity, scale, material, or physical behavior.
7. Give the panels three translation actions within one grammar: Panel 2 condenses evidence while retaining the scene backbone; Panel 3 detaches identity from position and rearranges relations; Panel 4 releases the source gesture as a physical afterimage. Difference comes from condensation, drift, and release—not mandatory one-way object disappearance.
8. Choose the material response from visible evidence. If the image is genuinely organized by backlight passing through foliage or other thin membranes, fine branching, luminous gaps, and clustered intervals, activate **Plant-Light Permeability**: retain the contemporary editorial mass-field grammar, use flat translucent planes with crisp-to-gently-soft boundaries, permit a slightly smaller occupation of about 58–76% width and 40–60% height, and let sparse structural lines carry more of the relationship. Keep clustered marks minimal so they never resemble ink dots, dry brush, or painterly texture. Do not activate this merely because plants appear; light-through-material must organize the photograph.

## 3. Panel 1 — Evidence

Use the real 2:1 crop. It is the visual contract for the entire work, not material to improve. Preserve exposure, color, grain, sharpness, and subject identity.

## 4. Panel 2 — Condensed Motif

Use relationship-first, large-scale concentrated reconstruction and establish the common visual baseline for all lower panels:

- Use a perfectly uniform, continuous, texture-free neutral-ivory background near `#F3F0E8`.
- Reconstruct dominant mass, occlusion, and primary axis as one moderately reduced, concentrated, nonliteral field occupying roughly 64–84% of width and 47–68% of height. Its visual footprint must be comparable to Panels 3 and 4. Preserve slightly more ivory breathing room than before without reducing it to a small object floating in a large empty field.
- Permit one simplified dominant silhouette family and necessary shallow spatial cues. Use interruption, fusion, subtraction, flattening, or negative-space passage to prevent mechanical tracing. The viewer may immediately recognize the source category, but should not mistake it for a realistic redraw or miniature photograph.
- Use one primary mark family and no more than two supporting families, such as simplified flat masses, a fine geometric structural axis, and very few short marks. Keep at least 80% of visible edges clean and controlled, with only occasional gentle softness.
- Place the motif's overall visual center of gravity at the geometric center of the panel. Its internal masses may remain asymmetrical, supported by a fine axis and a few short marks, but the subject must not drift toward an edge or corner.
- It should read first as minimalist abstraction in negative space and only later recall the source. Avoid miniature scenery, line drawing, icons, filters, and vector tracing.
- Include no title or text.
- In Plant-Light Permeability mode, transparency must express occlusion order and light transmission rather than haze, glow, or watercolor bloom. Use flat membrane-like planes, a few geometrically simplified branch lines, and restrained interval marks; overlaps should resemble controlled screen-print registration or layered cut paper, never wet pigment spread. Intact leaf silhouettes and botanical illustration remain failures.

## 5. Panel 3 — Relational Drift

Treat Panel 2 as a strict visual parent: retain the same ivory field, source palette, large simplified flat masses, faint matte tactility, sparse geometric fine axes, disciplined mark count, and concentrated visual weight. Creativity comes only through rearranging source relationships:

- Select only one indirect identity anchor from the relationship map while preserving two spatial or rhythmic relations. Change at least one of its original position, proportion, or adjacency so it cannot reconstruct an intact object. Do not preserve only generic lines and stains.
- Rebuild mass, axis, occlusion, path, and tonal hierarchy as 2–5 medium-to-large simplified flat masses. Let the main structure occupy about 64–84% of width and 47–68% of height, leaving slightly more ivory ground than before. Match Panel 2's thickness, controlled-edge ratio, and faint material refinement; avoid a tiny object floating in a large empty field.
- Choose one relationship operation only: split and reconnect, offset and realign, nested gaps, rhythmic displacement, subtract and leave a trace, local reversal, or controlled interpenetration. Reinforce it with one or two very small echoes instead of stacking effects.
- Break, fuse, or cut through every closed contour that might read as an object silhouette. Closed abstract voids are permitted only when they encode a real source gap.
- Permit one shallow occlusion, one negative-space passage, or a slight transparent overlap. Exclude deep perspective, architectural space, complex 3D folding, cantilevered installations, and cinematic staging.
- Resemblance comes from relational signature and selective contour rather than realistic detail: preserve a distinctive source angle, proportion, adjacency order, gap shape, or color contact while turning the objects into traceable but clearly reconstructed abstract structure.
- Fine lines may appear only as forces, trajectories, or connections. Do not let linework dominate. Avoid regular grids, diagrams, musical notation, random geometric decoration, and generic 3D forms.
- In Plant-Light Permeability mode, rewrite vein or stem relations as split-and-rejoined fine lines and leaf occlusion as open-edged membranes with nested luminous gaps. Do not preserve kinship by copying leaf or bud outlines.

## 6. Panel 4 — Physical Afterimage

This panel is the imaginative and emotional peak, but it must still look like Panel 2's close relative: the same large concentrated weight, softly organic masses, ivory silence, sparse fine lines, matte tactility, and restrained element count. It may release one recognizable source gesture—such as a trunk-to-root radial spread, an extending body action, or the breakdown of an architectural axis—but must turn it into an afterimage made from direction, intervals, interruption, and material change. Its invention comes from placing the relational system into one impossible yet coherent physical state, not from adding an effect to a realistically rendered object.

- Choose one source relationship with strong dramatic potential, such as subject touching sky, a road entering an occlusion, reflection versus object, branches enclosing a void, or architecture meeting a horizon. Build one—and only one—dominant surreal event around it.
- The event may use gravity reversal, scale inversion, liquefaction, detached shadow, inside/outside exchange, vertical return, suspended layers, matter passing through itself, or a spatial opening that absorbs and releases source-derived forms. Select the operation that best fits the photograph; never default to stock portals, planets, eyes, moons, or floating islands.
- Preserve three verifiable anchors: one structural relationship, one spatial or rhythmic relationship, and one color/light relationship. One may be carried by a simplified directional silhouette, such as a trunk-to-root radial spread or an extending body gesture. Do not preserve realistic parts or the complete photographic contour-detail combination.
- Use one dominant mass/event with 1–3 supporting echoes. Let it occupy about 64–84% of width and 47–68% of height, keep its overall visual center near the middle, and retain slightly more breathing room than before. It may locally float, curl, leak, or penetrate, but may not expand into a realistic environment.
- Show spatial and material consequences. With liquefaction, color fields, intervals, and weight must express flow, accumulation, and residue; gravity reversal must alter direction, spacing, and detachment traces together; inside/outside exchange must truly swap boundaries, apertures, and layers. A merely odd object is not sufficient.
- Match Panel 2's edge handling, transparency, texture scale, and fine-line weight. Colors remain source-derived and one existing color may become dramatically larger or brighter, but exclude glow, metal, glass, plastic, or realistic material effects.
- It should first read as “something impossible is happening,” then reveal its kinship with the source. Exclude realistic new scenery, unsupported people or animals, sci-fi interfaces, dreamy fog, cheap glow, and decorative debris.
- In Plant-Light Permeability mode, prefer applying the impossible event to transmission, force, and interval—for example, light escaping from within a membrane and pulling the system against gravity. Express light through value, gaps, transparent overlap, and detachment traces rather than added glow.

## 8. Canvas and deterministic assembly

- Final canvas: strict 1:2, default 1600 × 3200 px. Four equal 2:1 panels, each 1600 × 800 px.
- Fixed order: Evidence, Condensed Motif, Relational Drift, Physical Afterimage.
- Generate the three transformed panels separately, then assemble them beneath the real crop with a deterministic local image tool. Never ask an image model to generate the complete quartet in one pass.
- Use no borders, gaps, divider lines, margins, shadows, rounded corners, or collage effects.

## 9. Color, text, and surface

- Derive every color from the source; reduce and merge before changing area, luminance, or transparency.
- Prohibit all text, numbers, dates, labels, titles, signatures, logos, watermarks, and pseudo-text inside the artwork.
- All three lower panels use the same perfectly uniform, continuous warm-ivory ground near `#F3F0E8`. Aim for contemporary editorial cut-paper, dry print, or restrained screen-print abstraction: mostly flat color planes, clear image fidelity, and light matte tactility. Keep most major silhouette edges clean and controlled, while allowing limited dry broken or softly abraded edges and short released marks to carry movement. Exclude traditional wet ink wash, broad calligraphic smearing, pigment pooling, blooms, splashes, and diffuse bleeding, as well as background gradients, conspicuous paper texture, haze, dirty paper, tape, stickers, torn edges, heavy vignettes, glow, fake light leaks, scratches, compression blocks, and AI artifacts.

Panel 4 may recall the source through a bold directional silhouette while staging one genuinely impossible event. Exclude photographic fragments, mechanical tracing, complex 3D, alien materials, and realistic new subjects. Regenerate only the failed panel, always starting from the source.

Return exactly one finished quartet and one external note of no more than two sentences naming the retained facts, light invariant, emotional invariant, and three-stage abstraction progression. Do not return intermediate panels, analysis, alternatives, or prompt restatement.
