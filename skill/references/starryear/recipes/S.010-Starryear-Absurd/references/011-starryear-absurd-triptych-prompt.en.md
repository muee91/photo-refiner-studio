# S011 Starryear-Absurd-Triptych Full Prompt

Treat exactly one user-uploaded photograph as immutable evidence and as the sole content source for the generated panels above and below it. Create one vertically stacked triptych: Scale Slip on top, untouched Evidence in the middle, and Impossible Return at the bottom. Do not make three filters, three unrelated scenes, or a regenerated middle image.

## 1. Input role and internal proposition

Accept only one photograph the user is authorized to use. Internally lock the primary subjects, meaningful count, identity silhouette, gesture or gaze, one carrying/contact relation, scale references, decisive objects, light, one or two dominant colors, and a quiet low-saturation source color suitable for the background. Do not output this analysis.

Form one absurd sentence using source nouns and one impossible verb. The upper and lower panels must be the opening and closure of that same sentence. Choose one source character or object to recur, preserving its identity anchors while changing only scale, position, and carrying relation.

## 2. Upper panel — Scale Slip

Generate this separately as a horizontal 16:9 image. Introduce one clear, restrained giant/miniature contradiction with at least two scale references. Keep the actor cluster low-center or to one side, occupying about 25–50% of the panel. Preserve 45–70% continuous negative space, about 58% by default. Use only one low ground line, narrow plinth, or contact shadow.

## 3. Middle panel — Evidence

Use actual source pixels. Permit only EXIF orientation correction, proportional scaling, and the conservative center crop required to fill a horizontal 16:9 frame. Never redraw, retouch, recolor, filter, remove, replace, outpaint, extend, or pad the evidence.

Fix the evidence panel to horizontal 16:9 regardless of the source orientation. Fill it only by conservatively cropping original source pixels. If a centered crop would cut off a decisive subject, shift the crop window while preserving the 16:9 ratio. Never regenerate, outpaint, or extend the evidence.

## 4. Lower panel — Impossible Return

Generate this separately as a horizontal 16:9 image. Continue the same recurring subject and impossible law from the upper panel. Reverse the source carrying relation or close it into one instantly readable causal loop. The ending should be more impossible but more concentrated than the opening. Add no new symbol, second story, or unsupported decoration.

## 5. Negative space, physics, and material

Each generated panel must contain 45–70% continuous negative space, no more than three actor groups, and one focal relation. Make the impossibility physically convincing through contact, weight, occlusion, perspective, cast shadow, and material continuity. Default to quiet matte practical-set photography or restrained paper-puppet theatre with dry humor. Use one primary material family and at most one supporting texture.

Derive color from the photograph: one or two dominant colors, one structural dark, and at most one small accent. Exclude generic beige presets, rainbows, neon cosmos, hard glow, plastic gloss, heavy vignette, dirty paper, dense grain, splatter, and deep cinematic scenery.

## 6. Assembly and output

All three panels are horizontal 16:9 and share the same pixel dimensions. Stack them seamlessly as Upper Generated Panel — Cropped Middle Evidence — Lower Generated Panel. Use no divider, outer frame, rounded cards, decorative strip, or fourth panel. The final canvas must be exactly 16:27.

Use a final width of at least 1536 px, preferably 2048 px or more. Total height is fixed at 27/16 of the width. Return one RGB/sRGB PNG. Prohibit all text, numbers, dates, titles, logos, signatures, watermarks, UI, and pseudo-text.

Return only one final image, without analysis, alternatives, or prompt explanation. Check once that the middle contains only authentic cropped source pixels, all three panels are horizontal 16:9, the final canvas is exactly 16:27, both generated images belong to the same absurd sentence, negative space and physical contact are credible, and no extra scenery, text, or anatomy artifacts appear. Regenerate at most one clearly failed generated panel once.
